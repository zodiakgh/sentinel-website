Privacy notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This notice explains how the Sentinel Archive newsletter handles email addresses when someone asks to receive occasional updates about the Sentinel project.

The newsletter is optional. Do not subscribe someone else without their permission. A parent or legal guardian should review and approve the operation of this service before it is opened to the public.

1. Information collected

If you subscribe, the service stores your email address, subscription status, the date and time consent was recorded, confirmation status and technical tokens used to confirm or cancel the subscription. The service does not ask for a name, postal address or date of birth.

2. Why it is used

Your email address is used only to send occasional official announcements about Sentinel, such as a possible return or important project news. It is not used for advertising profiling or sold to other parties.

3. Consent and confirmation

Submitting the form records a pending request. Your subscription becomes active only after you click the confirmation link sent to your email. You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every newsletter.

4. Service providers

The newsletter uses Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 to operate the API and store subscriber records, and Resend to deliver confirmation and announcement emails. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services. Their own privacy and data-processing terms also apply.

5. Retention and deletion

Pending requests that are not confirmed should be periodically deleted. Active records are kept while the subscription remains active. When you unsubscribe, the record is marked as unsubscribed so that the address is not accidentally added back to future mailings. You may request deletion of your record through the contact link below.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact the project operator. You can always unsubscribe directly from an email.

7. Security

Administrative access is restricted using a private secret. The newsletter API uses HTTPS. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid sending sensitive information by email.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the project operator through the Sentinel contact profile.
