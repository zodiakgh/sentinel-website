Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Information we process

Sentinel Status processes your email address, account and workspace identifiers, monitor configuration, check results, incident content and status page settings. Security logs may include limited request metadata used to prevent abuse.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate users, run requested monitoring checks, display status pages, manage incidents, deliver transactional security emails and protect the service.

3. Infrastructure providers

The service is designed to use Cloudflare for application hosting and database storage, and Resend for transactional email delivery. The primary application database is configured for the European Union.

4. Public information

Status pages and incident updates you publish are public. Do not publish personal, confidential or sensitive information in public fields.

5. Retention and security

Detailed monitor checks are retained for up to 30 days in the initial configuration. Authentication codes expire after five minutes and are single-use. Session records expire automatically. We apply access controls and hashing to authentication data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

You may stop using the service and request help with account data through the support portal. We will update this policy when data practices materially change.
