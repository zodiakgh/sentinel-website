Privacy Policy

What personal data Sentinel handles, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to use your rights.

1.The short version

  • Sentinel is a Discord bot run by an individual in France. It processes Discord data (IDs, usernames, roles, messages the bot needs to act on) to provide the features that Server Administrators turn on.
  • The website has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It stores your language choice in your browser. If you log in or verify, it sets short-lived cookies.
  • Logging in with Discord shows you your servers; verifying on a server’s page lets that server give you a role. Discord’s access token is revoked immediately and never stored.
  • Most bot data is kept until it is deleted by Administrators or on request. You can ask for access, correction or deletion at any time at support@sentineldashboard.site; requests are answered within one month.
  • Your data is not sold. It is shared only with the services needed to run Sentinel, listed below.

2.Who is responsible

The Sentinel bot, the website and the login and verification service are operated by zxdac_, an individual acting in a non-professional capacity, based in France (see the legal notice). Contact: support@sentineldashboard.site. There is no data protection officer.

Roles differ by situation. For the website, the login and verification service, support requests, security and the operation of the Bot itself, the Operator decides why and how data is used and is the controller. For data about a community’s members that exists because a Server’s Administrators enabled a Module (for example warnings, tickets, levels or verification settings), the Administrators decide the purposes and means for their community and are controllers of that community data; the Operator provides the tool and processes the data on their behalf and under their configuration.

3.What this policy covers

This policy covers the Sentinel Discord bot, the website at sentineldashboard.site, the optional Discord login, the server verification page and communication with the Operator (e-mail and support server). It does not cover Discord, GitHub, Cloudflare or other services, which have their own policies, nor Servers’ own rules and notices.

5.Message content, presence and intents

The Bot asks Discord for the Server Members, Message Content, Presence, Voice States, Messages and Reactions intents. The Bot reads message content in real time so that AutoMod, leveling, counting, the honeypot trap and Flow triggers can work. Presence data is used for features such as roles based on a member’s status. In most cases content is evaluated in memory and not stored; where a Module stores content or an excerpt (for example ticket transcripts), it is described in the table above. The Bot does not read messages in order to build advertising profiles, and it does not sell or share them.

6.Website, cookies and local storage

As with any website, the hosting provider and the network necessarily process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested when you load a page; these are handled by GitHub and, for the login and verification service, by Cloudflare.

A few images are loaded from Discord’s servers (for example the team avatars and server icons), so Discord receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

To limit abuse, a keyed hash of your IP address (not the address itself) is kept for 60 seconds to allow one message per minute. The e-mail is kept in the Operator’s mailbox as long as needed to handle your request, and is deleted on request.

7.Login with Discord

The dashboard page can log you in with Discord (OAuth2) so it can show you the servers you manage. It requests the identify and guilds scopes only. It reads your Discord ID, username, display name, avatar and the list of servers you are in, and keeps only the servers you own or where you hold Manage Server or Administrator permission, with whether Sentinel is present there.

This information is stored for up to 24 hours under a random session identifier held in the sentinel_session cookie, so the page can show it. Discord’s access token is revoked right after your identity is read and is never stored. The login cannot read your messages or e-mail address, cannot join servers for you and cannot change your account or your servers. Log out on the dashboard page to delete the session immediately, and you can remove the authorisation in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.

8.Server verification page

When a Server uses Sentinel’s verification gate, its members are sent to a verification page on this website. To verify, you log in with Discord using only the identify scope. The service then reads your Discord ID, name and avatar, checks with the Bot whether you are a member of that Server and whether you already hold the role, applies the thresholds the Server set (minimum account age, which is derived from your Discord ID, and minimum time in the Server), and, if everything passes, gives you the verified role and removes the unverified role if one is set. If the Server enabled it, the Bot also posts a short message in the Server’s log channel saying that you verified.

The verification session lasts at most 15 minutes. The Discord token is revoked immediately and never stored. The service stores, per Server, the settings the Bot registered (see retention). A verification is not an identity check: it does not collect your real name, your age or any document.

10.Who receives data

  • Discord Inc.: everything you do on Discord, including the Bot’s API calls, is processed by Discord under its own policy.
  • DisHost (dishost.fr, France): hosts the Bot and its data on a Pterodactyl panel.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: runs the login and verification service (Workers and KV storage) and, if enabled, the Turnstile captcha. It acts as a processor.
  • GitHub, Inc.: hosts the static website (GitHub Pages) and receives technical data when you load it.
  • Server Administrators and staff: can see data about their own community that the Bot records (warnings, tickets, transcripts, levels, verification logs).
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, for example on a valid legal request.

Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

11.Third-party services used by commands

Some commands send what you type, or identifiers derived from it, to the services below. Only the commands you run trigger these requests, and their own policies apply.

  • Lavalink music node: search queries and track identifiers for music playback. By default this is a public third-party node set in the code.
  • OpenRouter and the underlying model provider: prompts sent to AI chat, and prompts used by AI server generation.
  • Pollinations: prompts for AI image generation.
  • Lookup and content services: depending on the command, TMDB, MusicBrainz, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, ArcGIS, GitHub, IP, RDAP and DNS lookup services, public SearXNG instances and image APIs, YouTube, TikTok and similar platforms.

Do not type secrets or sensitive personal data into commands that call external services.

12.Transfers outside the EEA

The Bot is hosted in France. Several recipients are established in or use infrastructure in the United States or elsewhere (Discord, Cloudflare, GitHub and several third-party services). Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, it relies on the safeguards those providers use, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, as described in their own documentation. You can ask the Operator for more information at the contact address.

14.Security

The Operator takes reasonable steps to protect data, and describes here only measures that exist:

  • The website and the login and verification service are served over HTTPS.
  • The Discord client secret and the bot credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the Worker’s environment and are never sent to the browser or published on the website.
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax. The login redirect is protected with a signed state value, and actions that change state check the request origin.
  • Discord access tokens are revoked immediately after use and are not stored. Sessions expire automatically.
  • Verification re-checks every condition on the server side; the browser cannot grant itself a role.

The Bot itself does not encrypt its data files at rest; they are protected by the hosting environment’s access controls. No system is perfectly secure, and the Operator cannot guarantee absolute security.

15.Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, and subject to the conditions the law sets, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability for data you provided, where the processing is based on consent or a contract and is automated;
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on it;
  • give instructions about your data after your death (France).

If you live elsewhere, you may have similar rights under the law of your country.

16.How to exercise your rights

Write to support@sentineldashboard.site, or contact the Operator on the support server. To protect your data from being handed to someone else, the Operator may ask you to prove that you control the Discord account concerned, for example by sending the request from that account. Please give your Discord user ID and, if you can, the Servers concerned.

Requests are answered within one month. Where the law allows, this period can be extended for complex requests, and you will be told. Requests are free unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive. Because most community data is controlled by Server Administrators, the Operator may need to involve them, or may direct you to them.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or to the authority of your country of residence.

17.Server administrators

If you manage a Server, you decide which Modules run there and what they record about your members. You are responsible for having a legal basis, for informing your members (for example about moderation logs, ticket transcripts, verification and levels), for answering their requests about data you control, and for not enabling features that you cannot justify. You can disable Modules, delete records with the Bot’s commands, remove the Bot, or ask the Operator to delete your Server’s data.

18.Automated tools

AutoMod, AntiNuke, verification and some other Modules take actions automatically when rules configured by a Server’s Administrators are met (for example deleting a message, timing out a member or withholding a role). They do not produce legal effects beyond what the Server’s own rules provide. The Operator does not make decisions about individuals. If you think an automated action was wrong, contact the Server’s staff, who can review and reverse it.

19.Children

Sentinel is meant for Discord users aged 13 or older, in line with Discord’s own minimum age, and the Operator does not knowingly collect data from younger children. Where the law of your country requires parental consent for a young person’s data to be processed (in France, under 15 for processing based on consent), a parent or guardian should agree on their behalf. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Service, write to the Operator and the data will be deleted.

20.Removing the bot and deleting data

Removing the Bot from a Server does not automatically erase everything it stored about that Server: configuration and records generally remain, except that notification subscriptions are removed. To have a Server’s data deleted, an Administrator can use the Bot’s commands to clear module data where they exist, or write to the Operator. To have your own personal data deleted, follow the “How to exercise your rights” section. Deletion applies to the Bot’s own files and to the login and verification service; copies held by Discord or by other third parties are governed by their policies.

21.Data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to create a risk for people, the Operator will notify the competent authority and, where the law requires it, the people affected, within the deadlines the law sets.

22.Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the Service evolves. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of this page, and significant changes may also be announced on the support server.

23.Contact

For anything about your data: support@sentineldashboard.site, or the support server linked in the footer of every page. Operator: zxdac_, France. Requests are answered within one month.
