3. Retention and security

The code does not define general retention periods: most data stays until a server administrator removes it or the files are deleted, with exceptions such as the AutoMod incident cap. The bot does not implement encryption at rest; protection depends on the hosting environment.

In practice, the operator does not delete stored data on a schedule: it stays until a server administrator or the operator removes it. Updates to the bot can temporarily disable some functionality. The bot is hosted in France by DisHost (dishost.fr) on a Pterodactyl panel.

4. Third parties that receive data

  • Discord — All interaction with Discord (messages, members, voice state). Governed by Discord’s own privacy policy.
  • Lavalink node (music) — Search queries and track identifiers are sent to the configured Lavalink node, which by default is a public third-party node set in the code.
  • OpenRouter (AI) — Prompts you send to AI chat, and prompts used by AI server generation, are sent to OpenRouter and the underlying model provider.
  • Image generation — Prompts for AI image generation are sent to the public Pollinations endpoint.
  • Lookup & content services — Queries typed into commands may be forwarded to services such as TMDB, MusicBrainz, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, ArcGIS, GitHub, IP/RDAP/DNS lookup services, public SearXNG instances and image APIs, YouTube and TikTok.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights of access, correction and deletion. Ask the operator through the contact above, or on the support server. Server administrators can also remove their server’s data by removing modules’ configuration or the bot.

Requests are answered within one month. Write to support@sentineldashboard.site or use the support server.

6. Children

Sentinel is intended for Discord users aged 13 or older, in line with Discord’s minimum age. We do not knowingly target younger children.

7. This website

This static site has no accounts, forms or analytics of its own; the optional Discord login on the Dashboard page is described below. It stores your language choice in your browser’s local storage, and loads the two team avatars from Discord’s CDN, which may receive your IP address as a normal part of loading an image. Links to Discord leave this site.
