11.Rules specific to certain features

Moderation, AutoMod and AntiNuke

These Modules can delete messages, time out, kick or ban members, change roles, lock channels or the whole server, and restore deleted structure. Detection is automatic and can be wrong (false positives and false negatives). Administrators must test their configuration (for example in monitor mode) and are responsible for the resulting actions. No protective feature guarantees that an attack or abuse will be stopped.

Verification

The verification gate can require members to confirm their identity as a Discord account through the Website, optionally applying checks such as minimum account age or minimum time in the server, and then assigns a role. Verification is not proof of a person’s real identity, age or good intent, and Administrators must not rely on it as such.

Tickets and transcripts

Tickets can generate transcripts containing the messages exchanged in them. Anyone opening a ticket should assume that the Server’s staff can read it and that a transcript may be kept by the Server. Do not share secrets, passwords or payment details in tickets.

Music

Music playback relies on third-party music nodes and sources. You are responsible for having the right to play the content you request in your community, and the Operator does not host or license any music. Availability depends on third parties.

AI-assisted features

Some commands send your input to third-party AI or image-generation providers. Output may be inaccurate, offensive or unsuitable, and must not be relied on for legal, medical, financial or safety decisions. Do not submit sensitive personal data. AI-generated server structures or images are suggestions that Administrators must review.

Lookup and information commands

These commands query public sources. Results can be incomplete or wrong. You are solely responsible for how you use them and for complying with the law (including data-protection law) when you do.

Backups, snapshots and restores

Notifications and automations

Notifications depend on external platforms that can change, throttle or block access without notice. Flow and other automations execute exactly what an Administrator configures; the Administrator is responsible for their effects.
