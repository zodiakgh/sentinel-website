Commands, at a glance.

The core command map for running a music session. Exact options can vary with the version deployed on your bot host.

Music commands

CommandPurpose
/playSearch for music or play a supported URL.
/queueView the tracks waiting to play.
/nowplayingShow information about the current track.
/panelOpen the interactive music controls.
/volumeChange the playback volume.
/leaveDisconnect from the voice channel.
This is the intended core command set for Sentinel Music. Check the slash-command list in your Discord server for the commands currently registered by your deployed version.

Player controls

The player panel is intended to keep the common playback actions together:

  • Pause and resume the current track.
  • Skip to the next queued track.
  • Stop playback and clear the current session.
  • Inspect the queue and adjust volume.

Audio effects

Effects such as Bass Boost, 8D, Nightcore or equalizer presets require compatible Lavalink plugins. A public node may not include them, and its available search providers can change.

What playback needs

  • The bot must have permission to connect and speak in the voice channel.
  • A reachable Lavalink v4 node is required for audio playback.
  • Keep the bot token and Lavalink password in private environment variables.
