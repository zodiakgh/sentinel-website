Security & community

Protection that stays out of the way.

Sentinel combines preventive tools, such as verification, AutoMod, AntiNuke and a lockdown, with the community features that keep a server alive: tickets, welcome messages, levels, giveaways and temporary voice rooms.

Most modules are configured from inside Discord with guided panels and setup wizards, so administrators do not need a separate dashboard.

  • 10 AutoMod modules — 8 actions, exemptions, presets, incident log
  • 10 AntiNuke protections — per-module thresholds and time windows
  • Verification gate — verified/unverified roles, account-age check
  • Backups & snapshots — save, compare and restore server structure
